# Week 1: Data Science Foundations & Statistical Concepts

## 1. What is Data Science?
Data Science is an interdisciplinary field combining domain knowledge, programming skills, and mathematics/statistics to extract actionable insights from structured and unstructured data.

### The Data Science Workflow:
1. **Problem Definition:** Understanding business objectives.
2. **Data Collection:** Gathering data via databases, APIs, or scraping.
3. **Data Cleaning:** Handling missing values, duplicates, and outliers.
4. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Detecting patterns and distributions.
5. **Modeling:** Applying statistical or machine learning models.
6. **Communication:** Visualizing findings and business reporting.

---

## 2. Variables & Data Types
- **Categorical (Qualitative):**
  - *Nominal:* Labels without natural order (e.g., Department, Gender).
  - *Ordinal:* Categorical with meaningful order (e.g., Education Level, Rating).
- **Numerical (Quantitative):**
  - *Discrete:* Countable whole numbers (e.g., Number of items, Age in years).
  - *Continuous:* Measurable values on a scale (e.g., Salary, Height, Revenue).

---

## 3. Core Statistics & Metrics
- **Measures of Central Tendency:**
  - **Mean:** Arithmetic average of values.
  - **Median:** Middle value dividing sorted data into halves (robust to outliers).
  - **Mode:** Most frequently occurring value.
- **Measures of Dispersion:**
  - **Range:** Difference between Maximum and Minimum.
  - **Variance ($S^2$):** Average squared difference from the mean.
  - **Standard Deviation ($\sigma$):** Square root of variance, indicating spread around the mean.
  - **Interquartile Range (IQR):** $Q_3 - Q_1$, used for identifying outliers.
